1. SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTION REFUND TERMS
Enterprise software licenses and team subscriptions may be refunded within 14 days of purchase upon manager approval.
Refund requests must be processed through the Finance Service Desk accompanied by invoice proof and manager authorization.

2. TRAVEL & MEAL ALLOWANCES
Domestic business travel per diem allowance for meals is $75 per day ($100 per day for high-cost tier cities like San Francisco and New York).
Flight bookings for trips under 6 hours must be Economy Class. Business Class requires Senior Vice President approval.

3. RECEIPT SUBMISSION & AUDIT COMPLIANCE
Receipts are required for all individual expenses exceeding $25.
Expense reports submitted after 45 days from the date of expenditure will be automatically rejected and ineligible for reimbursement.
Reimbursements are disbursed directly into employee bank accounts on the 15th and 30th of each month.
