===============================================================================
GLOBAL IT SECURITY PROTOCOLS & INFRASTRUCTURE STANDARDS v2.1
CLASSIFICATION: CONFIDENTIAL (LEVEL 2)
DEPARTMENT: SECURITY ENGINEERING
===============================================================================

1. IDENTITY & AUTHENTICATION PROTOCOLS
Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is strictly mandatory across all enterprise systems including GitHub, AWS, and Slack.
Password complexity rules require a minimum length of 16 characters including uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special symbols.
Passwords must be rotated automatically every 90 days via HashiCorp Vault.

2. SECURE ACCESS & SSH KEY ROTATION
Direct root access to production servers is strictly prohibited.
All administrative server access must pass through Teleport Bastion hosts.
SSH keys must be rotated every 90 days. Short-lived certificate-based SSH access is enforced for developer environments.

3. INCIDENT RESPONSE & SEVERITY SLAS
Severity 1 (Critical Data Breach / Service Outage): Response SLA < 15 minutes. Incident Commander assigned immediately.
Severity 2 (High Risk Vulnerability): Response SLA < 2 hours. Patching required within 24 hours.
Severity 3 (Medium Risk Issue): Response SLA < 24 hours. Resolution within 7 business days.

4. DATA CLASSIFICATION LEVELS
Level 1 (Public / Internal): Employee handbooks, general policy docs.
Level 2 (Confidential): Architecture diagrams, source code, internal APIs. Access restricted to technical staff.
Level 3 (Restricted / PII): Customer PII, billing databases, financial records. Requires Legal & VP Security approval.
